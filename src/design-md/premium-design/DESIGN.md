# Premium Design — Design Reference

> Refined dark luxury with restrained gold accents, polished spacing, and quiet confidence.

**Theme:** dark
**Flavor:** Premium Design

## Visual Character
Create value through restraint, material contrast, and confident whitespace.

## Tokens
Canvas is deep espresso black. Text is warm ivory. Accent is muted gold. Cards are dark glass with soft lift.

## Components
Buttons are elegant and measured. Cards need breathing room. Badges should feel like fine labels.

## Do's and Don'ts
Do use subtle material cues and strong copy. Do not add generic shine or loud luxury effects.
